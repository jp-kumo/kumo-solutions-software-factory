## Core Synthesis & Playbook Slices

### 1. The Autonomous Video Factory
- **Strategy:** Move from "time-per-video" to "one-time setup" cost.
- **The 2026 Ghost Stack:**
    - **Brain:** n8n (self-hosted for unlimited executions).
    - **Trigger:** Gemini agent scanning for trending Amazon/SaaS products.
    - **Voice:** ElevenLabs (mentor-style voice).
    - **Visuals:** Leonardo AI / Sora.
    - **Assembly:** Shotstack API.
- **Hook Logic:** Generate 5 variations of the first 3 seconds to "force" the algorithm to find buyers.

### 2. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- **Framework:** Lead with a 40–60 word definitive summary ("Answer First").
- **Schema 3.0:** Use JSON-LD to act as a "translator" for AI agents.
- **Entity Association:** Semantically link your brand to industry giants in the same context to train vector databases.

### 3. High-Compliance Recurring Assets
- **SaaS Focus:** AI Productivity (n8n), Cybersecurity, B2B Operations. "Integration angles" (showing how tools talk to each other) drive the highest conversions.
- **Fintech Precision:** Use Direct API integrations and Webhooks to track "funded accounts," not just "leads." Use AI monitoring (e.g., Fintel Check) to ensure compliance.

### 4. Intent-Driven Email (The "ATM")
- **Automated 5-Step Nurture:** Value Dump → Social Proof → Logic Bridge → Direct Ask → Pivot.
- **Lead Recovery:** Trigger emails based on "Site Search Abandonment" (e.g., searching for a product but not clicking).
