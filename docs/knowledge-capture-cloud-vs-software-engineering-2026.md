## Why this matters for Jacques
This content validates Jacques's current career pivot strategy. It provides the exact "language of value" needed for recruiter conversations and Kumo Solutions positioning.

### Key Alignment Points:
1. **The "Infrastructure Shift":** Companies are moving from "hiring feature builders" to "hiring system architects." Jacques's target roles (Cloud + AI Engineering) are in the growth sector, while entry-level pure software engineering is being "squeezed" by AI.
2. **AI Needs the Cloud:** Cloud engineers are the "landlords" of AI. Whether AI thrives or disappoints, the infrastructure (AWS, Terraform, CI/CD) remains a non-negotiable requirement.
3. **The "Clean Up" Role:** As non-technical teams use AI to ship more code/apps, the need for professional cloud engineers to manage the resulting security, cost, and architecture sprawl increases.

## Strategic Insights for Portfolio & Kumo

### 1. The Value is in the "Why," not the "How"
- **Insight:** Tutorials and certifications are baseline. Differentiation comes from reasoning through **trade-offs** and **architecture decisions**.
- **Jacques's Action:** Every project in the portfolio needs an "Architecture Decisions" log (`decisions.md`) explaining *why* a specific AWS service or security gate was chosen over alternatives.

### 2. High-Signal Skill Stack (2026)
The transcript lists the "six-figure cloud engineer" essentials:
- **AWS** (Core platform)
- **Terraform** (Infrastructure as Code - Jacques's next cert target)
- **CI/CD Pipelines** (The bridge to production)
- **Python & Bash** (The glue)
- **Networking & Security** (Day one fundamentals)
- **AI Infrastructure** (The growth engine)

### 3. "Invisible Unemployment" Hedge
- **Insight:** Pure software roles are seeing "invisible unemployment"—jobs just aren't coming back because AI handles the grunt work.
- **Jacques's Strategy:** Position as the "Cloud + AI Systems Engineer" who ensures the AI actually works reliably in production.
