## Why this matters for Jacques
Jacques needs a way to build authority for **Kumo Solutions** and his personal **Cloud/AI Portfolio** without sacrificing the 40+ hours a week required for deep engineering work. This system provides the "High-Volume, Low-Effort" framework to keep his LinkedIn/Twitter active.

### Key Alignment Points:
1. **Batching over Interruption:** Jacques prefers "built progress" and focused work blocks. Moving content from a daily chore to a once-a-month, 4-hour batch fits his mental model.
2. **Authority Building:** To land the March 2026 hiring season roles, Jacques needs a consistent trail of "Tutorials" and "Case Studies" (as identified in the prompt categories).
3. **Multi-Platform Leverage:** This batch shows how one technical project (e.g., the Secure Health RAG project) can be optimized into a LinkedIn technical post, a Twitter thread, and a broader Facebook story in minutes.

---

## The "Content Factory" System

### Step 1: Batch Ideation (30 Min)
- **Prompt Logic:** Ask for a specific mix (10 Problem-solving, 8 Tutorials, 5 Case Studies, 4 Motivational, 3 Reflections).
- **Thematic flow:** Organize into 4 weekly themes that build from *Foundation* to *Advanced*.

### Step 2: Drafting & Editing (2 Hours)
- **The "80/20" Rule:** AI provides 80% (structure/draft) in 30 seconds; Jacques provides the final 20% (personal stories/accuracy) in 5 minutes.
- **Critical Caveat:** Never post verbatim. Personal expertise is the only differentiator in a saturated market.

### Step 3: Platform Multiplier (1 Hour)
- **LinkedIn:** Professional, actionable, mobile-formatted.
- **Twitter:** Thread-based, high-hook first tweet.
- **Instagram/FB:** Visual-first or storytelling/personal angle.

---

## Strategic Implementation for Jacques

### The "Kumo Weekly Update" Workflow
**Concept:** Every month, Jacques picks 4 technical milestones he achieved (e.g., "AWS Bedrock integration," "LangGraph retry logic").
- **Week 1:** Case study on the "Why."
- **Week 2:** Tutorial on the "How."
- **Week 3:** Advanced lesson on "Failure/Retry logic."
- **Week 4:** Reflection on "Cloud vs AI Engineering."

**Action:** I can assist by taking his `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` logs and drafting the monthly batch based on these themes.
