### 3. AI FinOps & Runtime Optimization
**For:** Enterprises or startups seeing runaway LLM API bills.
**What we do:** We optimize your **OpenClaw** or agentic runtime using advanced prompt caching, model routing (High/Medium/Low tiers), and local heartbeat offloading.
**Outcome:** Immediate reduction in spend (60-90% target) without sacrificing system intelligence.

**For:** Legal, insurance, or pharmaceutical teams needing 100% auditable AI document extraction.

**Outcome:** A high-trust, auditable AI tool that complies with governance standards.
